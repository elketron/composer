PROPOSAL: LANDSCAPE, AND HIDE CHAT.

Turn the phone, keep the chat.

Today the app stacks the portrait column on its side and the chat gets a sliver. Proposed: chat on the left under a one-row header, the live view on the right at full height, folding into a rail when you want the width. Or hide the chat and give the view the screen.

ONE PALETTE, ONE SHAPE

Exact supplied Noema colors on both. The desktop takes the phone's small corners; the phone keeps its layout and type, only its colors change.

SHARED COLORS, NATIVE LAYOUTS

Desktop overlays become phone pages and bottom sheets. Keep 44px targets, small card radii and newest-first conversation on phone.

DESIGN BEFORE CODE

These are proposals, not shipped UI. Blender is schematic. No GPUI, Flutter or protocol code is changed.
